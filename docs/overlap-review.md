# Plan 02 overlap review

This review treats categories as navigation structures rather than mutually exclusive truths. Shared concepts should normally be represented once and cross-listed through `relatedCategories`; concepts remain separate when the underlying preference, perspective, or semantic meaning is materially different.

## Priority overlap decisions

| Pair | Shared canonical concepts | Decision |
|---|---:|---|
| Power Exchange / Relationship Dynamics | 25 | Keep one canonical concept for each relationship-scoped power dynamic. Relationship-oriented concepts remain primarily owned by `relationship_dynamics` and are cross-listed in `power_exchange`. |
| Power Exchange / Psychological Play | 20 | Keep one canonical concept for negotiated psychological-control patterns. They remain primarily owned by `psychological_play` and are cross-listed in `power_exchange`. Emotional-state concepts remain separate when they measure the feeling itself rather than the interaction pattern. |
| Roleplay / Fantasy & Transformation | 17 | Keep transformation/fantasy themes canonical under `fantasy_transform` and cross-list them in `roleplay` when enactment/role framing is relevant. Do not collapse distinct fictional themes into one generic roleplay item. |
| Humiliation / Objectification | 14 | Keep shared humiliation/objectification manifestations as single canonical concepts. Preserve separate emotional-arousal concepts where the questionnaire is measuring the feeling/reaction rather than the dynamic. |
| Bondage / Rope | 12 | Rope-specific concepts remain canonical under `rope_bondage` and are cross-listed under general `bondage_restraint`. General non-rope restraint remains distinct. |
| Impact / Pain | 12 | Impact implements/styles remain canonical under `impact_play` and are cross-listed under `pain_play`. Non-impact pain interests remain independently answerable. |
| Remote / Exhibition | 5 | Added canonical cross-references for `remote_camera_play`, `video_call_play`, `photo_exchange_private`, `video_exchange_private`, and `remote_observation`. Remote communication and non-remote observation remain distinct where neither implies the other. |
| Orgasm Control / Chastity | 6 | Chastity concepts remain canonical under `chastity` and are cross-listed under `orgasm_control`. Remote-control relevance is additionally discoverable from `remote_digital` where applicable. |

## Exact-label duplicates reviewed

Four exact normalized label groups remain in the Plan 01 editorial warning queue. They are not silently merged in this navigation-only plan:

- `rope_bondage` / `rope_bondage_general`: category gate versus detailed answerable concept; separate roles are intentional.
- `obedience` / `emotion_obedience`: interaction dynamic versus emotional arousal state; semantically distinct.
- `objectification_general` / `emotion_objectification`: dynamic versus emotional arousal state; semantically distinct.
- `anal_beads` / `anal_beads_device`: likely consolidation candidate, but the two legacy IDs currently carry different detail-profile/body-binding metadata. Merging them safely belongs with the semantic/dynamic-detail work so existing detailed answers are not reinterpreted.

## Deferred to later plans

This review intentionally does not convert subtypes into adaptive details, redesign semantic dimensions, or rewrite labels/descriptions. Those changes belong to Plans 03, 04, and 08. The taxonomy added here is designed so those later migrations can happen without changing navigation identity again.
